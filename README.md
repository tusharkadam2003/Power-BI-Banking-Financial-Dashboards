# 🏦 Power BI Banking & Financial Analytics Dashboards 📊

A collection of interactive **Power BI dashboards** designed to analyze
banking operations, credit risk, loan portfolios, collection
performance, and legal recovery activities. These dashboards turn
financial and operational data into meaningful insights through KPI
tracking, interactive visualizations, and business performance analysis.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases five Power BI dashboards covering different
aspects of banking and financial operations.

The objective is to monitor business performance, identify high-risk
accounts, track loan outstanding amounts, evaluate collection
efficiency, analyze IVR operations, and monitor legal cases and recovery
performance.

## 📊 Dashboard Collection

### 1. IVR Operations Dashboard 📞

Analyzes Interactive Voice Response (IVR) operations to understand call
volumes, customer interactions, skillset performance, recognition rates,
and call termination patterns.

**Key Metrics** - Total Calls - Unique Customers - Average Call Time -
Recognition Rate - IVR Termination Rate - Skillset Termination Rate -
Repeat Call Rate

**Key Features** - Daily Call Volume Trends - Call Distribution by
Recognition Status - Skillset Performance Analysis - IVR Option
Performance Summary - Customer Type Distribution - Top IVR Termination
Reasons - Interactive filters for date, customer type, skillset, and
recognition status

**Business Objective:** Improve call handling efficiency, identify
termination patterns, and evaluate IVR performance.

### 2. NBFC Credit & Risk Dashboard 🏦

Provides an overview of credit exposure, loan approvals, disbursements,
outstanding balances, overdue amounts, and customer risk profiles.

**Key Metrics** - Total Applications - Approval Rate - Total Sanctioned
Amount - Total Disbursed Amount - Total Outstanding Amount - Total
Overdue Amount - Overdue Ratio - Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Ratio

**Key Features** - Risk Profile Overview - Early Warning System (EWS)
Analysis - Overdue Ratio Gauge - NPA Ratio Gauge - Top Risk Accounts
Table - Days Past Due (DPD) Analysis - Security Coverage and Legal
Action Indicators

**Business Objective:** Monitor credit risk exposure, identify
potentially risky accounts, and support proactive risk management.

### 3. Loan Operations Portfolio Dashboard 💰

Analyzes the loan portfolio, outstanding balances, disbursements,
collections, overdue accounts, and payment methods.

**Key Metrics** - Active Loans - Total Outstanding - Total Disbursed -
Total Collections - Overdue Outstanding - NPA Ratio

**Key Features** - Outstanding Amount by Product - Disbursement and
Collection Trends - Payment Mode Distribution - Top Risk Accounts -
Product-wise Collection Analysis - Loan Account Details - DPD
Monitoring - Date, product, and branch filters

**Business Objective:** Evaluate portfolio performance, monitor
repayment behavior, and identify accounts requiring collection
follow-up.

### 4. Collection Department Dashboard 📈

Focuses on collection efficiency, overdue accounts, pending amounts,
customer risk, and collection channel performance.

**Key Metrics** - Total Outstanding - Amount Collected - Collection
Gap - Average Days Past Due (DPD) - Pending Amount - High-Risk Customers

**Key Features** - DPD Bucket Collection Analysis - Channel-wise
Collection Performance - Region-wise Performance - Product-wise
Collection Analysis - Geographic Performance Map - Regional Filters -
Outstanding and Collection Comparisons

**Business Objective:** Identify collection gaps, compare collection
channels, monitor overdue accounts, and improve recovery performance.

### 5. Legal Department Dashboard ⚖️

Monitors legal cases, case statuses, outstanding amounts, recovery
amounts, upcoming hearings, and pending legal actions.

**Key Metrics** - Total Cases - Active Cases - Closed Cases -
Outstanding Amount - Recovery Amount - Recovery Rate

**Key Features** - Case Status Overview - Cases by Legal Type - Cases by
Current Stage - Recovery by Legal Type - Upcoming Hearings and Pending
Actions - Case Status Indicators - Recovery Rate Gauge - Filters for
case type, court name, case status, and date

**Business Objective:** Track legal recovery activities, monitor pending
cases, prioritize upcoming hearings, and evaluate recovery performance.
